# Scoped governance

For versions or conflicts, read [evolution-review.md](evolution-review.md) first.
Keep each governance operation within the requested resource set.

## Crystals

Treat these as independent axes:

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| Synthesis review | Has the owner reviewed the synthesis? |
| Claim trust | Is each claim asserted, proposed, explored, or otherwise qualified? |
| Lifecycle and currency | Is this current knowledge, retained history, or an explicit removal? |

Read the full synthesis and its `CRYSTALLIZED_FROM` sources.
Missing provenance in one response is not proof that no source exists.
Use supported graph or source reads before that conclusion.

Editing a crystal's title or content automatically confirms its synthesis.
Include that effect in the proposed change and stay within the authorized review scope.
Do not confirm every crystal, assert every confirmed claim, or use trust as a stale marker.
Do not claim to speak for the owner when a private preference remains uncertain.
Apply any active owner rule for concrete-list review before bulk changes.

Preserve distinct facets and source records needed by active crystals.
Same titles or high similarity alone do not establish replacement.
Do not remove raw sources as an automatic part of crystal review.

## Lifecycle and routing

Archive, deprecate, restore, and forget are separate dispositions.
Use their supported contracts. Do not manually change visibility to simulate EVOLVES.

When routing unique material before an authorized archive:

1. Read and record the useful content and source attribution.
2. Verify the destination and its owner.
3. Confirm that the destination preserves the required claims and dates.
4. Perform only the authorized lifecycle change.
5. Read back the destination and source state.

Useful procedure memories remain supported.
Do not impose a skills-only ownership model on every user.
Rules hold short standing instructions at an appropriate owner, agent, or Space scope.
Working Memory holds current focus.

## Labels, entities, and ontology

Reuse existing labels. Follow the active owner's label limit and scope rules.
Progression can inherit labels; inspect the final set.

Before a merge or deletion, inspect affected resources and supported previews.
Similar names can have different meanings.
Zero use in one response does not prove zero use across resource types or Spaces.
Apply the exact supported plan rather than a force flag.
Refresh communities only for an authorized purpose, not as automatic cleanup.

Ontology drafts are proposals.
Inspect aliases, affected counts, examples, and actual link definitions.
A broad type must not become a narrow category because a draft suggests it.
Prefer a targeted correction to a global retype when it meets the request.
